In-class and/or Lecture Topics FLEX

Note to instructors

This is a flex/suggested list of topics that may be helpful to cover during in-class active learning, lectures, and/or class discussions.

  • Introduction to Rhetorical Genre
  • Rhetorical Situation
  • Finding Online Writing Samples in Your Field
  • The Writing Process
  • Academic Writing (looking at sub-genres of essays and analysis essays)
  • Prewriting and Working Outlines
  • Paragraphing
  • Revision (high and middle-order concern revision)
  • Design Basics and/or Visual Rhetoric
  • AI and Writing
  • Tone, Style, and Remixing Writing
  • Proofreading and Later Order Concerns
