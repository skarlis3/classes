Final Reflection

Note to instructors

Assign the reflection reading before this, with enough runway for students to reread their own work. WA3 and WA8 exist to give them something to reread — students who did those have material; students who didn't will write generalities. The question list is a prompt bank, not a structure, and the assignment says so twice because students default to answering it as a questionnaire.

Overview

You'll write a paper reflecting on your learning across this whole course — what you brought in, what happened, and what you're taking with you.

Cite specific assignments, discussions, readings, and moments from the course throughout. A reflection that could have been written about any class isn't a reflection.

Prompt

Reflect honestly on what you've learned. The strongest reflections deal with all three stages: before, during, and after.

The questions below are a starting point. Use the ones that get you thinking — do not write your paper as a list of questions and answers.

Before

  • What did you already know about writing coming into this course? What were your previous experiences with it?
  • What connections can you make between what you already knew and what you learned here?

During

  • How did you approach this course? Did your approach work?
  • What surprised you? What were the strongest moments of learning, and what made them strong?
  • What was hardest, and how did you get through it?
  • What did you learn about writing? About how you work?

After

  • Now that it's over, what's your overall reaction to the course?
  • What's the most important thing you learned about yourself? What will you carry forward?
  • What will you do the same way in future courses? What will you do differently?
  • How might you have better supported your classmates?

The goal is not to answer every question in order. It's to genuinely look back at what happened and make sense of it.

Building Toward It

Two earlier activities exist to give you raw material. Go back and reread what you wrote:

  • WA3: Process Freewrite, right after Project 1
  • WA8: Self-Assessment, right after Project 3

Format

  • MLA style
  • Minimum 1,000 words, in multiple paragraphs
  • Original title
