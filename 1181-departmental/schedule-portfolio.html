Example 16-Week Schedule (Portfolio Model)

Example schedule shown for a T/Th class. This is a suggested pacing guide — instructors should adapt it to fit their section.

In the portfolio model, students submit a final revised version of their genre analysis essay bundled with the final reflection at the end of the semester. This gives students an additional revision cycle after receiving feedback on their revised draft. Grading suggestion: use completion-based grading through drafts, then grade the final portfolio on criteria.

Week 1 — FLEX

  • Introduce the course, expectations, syllabus
  • Recommendations: student success topics, notetaking strategies, writing about previous writing experiences, introductions
  • Since students may still be adding/dropping this week, this week is designed to be flexible.

Week 2: Rhetorical Situation

  • 3.2 Rhetorical Situation — CORE
  • WA1: Rhetorical Situation Observation — FLEX

Week 3: Genre & GenAI Literacy

  • 3.3 Navigating Genres — CORE
  • GenAI & Writing Studies reading — CORE (PLACEHOLDER — OER in development)
  • Introduce Project 1: Genre Analysis Essay
  • WA2: Genre Observation — FLEX
  • WA3: GenAI & Course Policies — FLEX

Week 4: Choosing a Field

  • WA4: Field / Career Selection — FLEX

Week 5: Exploring Your Field

  • WA5: Field Exploration — FLEX

Week 6: Genre Selection

  • Narrow to one genre for the essay
  • WA6: Genre Selection & Initial Analysis — FLEX

Week 7: Sample Collection & Conventions

  • Collect multiple samples, begin identifying conventions
  • WA7: Sample Collection & Observations — FLEX

Week 8: Visual Rhetoric

  • 6.1 What are Multimodal Texts? — CORE
  • 6.2 Understanding Visual Rhetoric — CORE
  • WA8: Visual Rhetoric Observation — FLEX

Visual rhetoric is introduced here so that students have this framework before they begin writing their essay — especially useful for students analyzing visual or multimodal genres (websites, brochures, infographics, etc.).

Week 9: Academic Writing & Drafting

  • 3.4 Academic Writing as a Genre — CORE
  • 3.6 Understanding Essays — CORE
  • 4.5 Revising & Orders of Concern — CORE
  • WA9: Academic Writing — FLEX
  • Discuss essay organization, paragraphing, using evidence from samples
  • Begin drafting

Week 10: Rough Draft & Peer Review

  • WA10: Peer Review Preparation — FLEX
  • Rough Draft due — CORE
  • Peer Review — CORE

Week 11: Revised Draft Due & Project 2 Introduction

  • Revised Draft due — CORE
  • Reflection & Checklist — FLEX
  • Introduce Project 2: Multimodal Project

  • Return feedback on revised drafts

Week 13: Project 2 Work

  • (Project 2 work — instructor fills as needed)

Week 14: Project 2 Due / Presentations

  • Project 2 due — CORE

Week 15: Final Portfolio Work

  • Final draft revision and final reflection work
  • (Instructor fills as needed — conferences, workshops, drop-in help)
